MIRAGE
A traveler is walking toward you. They must not reach you. They must not stop walking either.
Drag — your image drifts to your finger
Tap — become water, palms, or a city
Cycle forms to keep them believing
the well
They walked far enough. Off to the side, where you had never been,
a real spring waited under a real stone.

You dissolved without being thanked.
the reaching
They walked into you and there was nothing there —
only the sand, warm and endless, and the sound
of their palms falling open.

The illusion cannot be embraced.
the sitting
They stopped believing. They sat down in the sand
and closed their eyes and the desert accepted them
the way it accepts everything, gently and without fuss.

You should have changed shape.
